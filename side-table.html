Side Table

Four helical strands, each of which touches both of its neighbours for an instant and supports them, twist upwards to hold the table top. From whatever direction you look, the combination of the four helical legs will show you a different shape. Light reflects dynamically from the alternating grain directions of the wood on the table surface.
